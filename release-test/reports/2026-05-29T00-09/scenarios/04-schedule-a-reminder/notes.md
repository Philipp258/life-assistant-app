# 04 — Schedule a reminder

**Status:** green

## Tried

- Asked Ada to remind Alex about a car repair appointment on Wednesday, June 3, 2026 at 15:00, the morning before at 09:00.
- Opened the created task from the chat card.

## Worked

- The assistant created a scheduled task titled “Remind Alex about car repair appointment.”
- Chat card and assistant reply showed Tuesday, June 2 at 09:00.
- Task detail showed the correct start time and the reminder description.

## Friction

- The scheduled task detail displayed status as “Running” even though it was a future scheduled reminder. The date/time was correct, but the status label is a little confusing.

## Rating

Green. The reminder was actually scheduled for the requested time and was easy to inspect.
