# 13 — Open exploration

**Goal.** After the scripted scenarios, spend a little time following
your curiosity through the app.

**Persona.** A careful release tester who has now used the main flows
and wants to poke at anything that looked interesting, fragile,
unfinished, or surprisingly good.

**Done when.** You have explored a few things that caught your
attention, or you have deliberately decided nothing else seems worth
checking in this run.

**Rate.** Use judgment. Record anything notable you found, especially
quality issues, confusing behavior, UI rough edges, regressions,
surprising strengths, or areas that deserve a dedicated future
scenario.
