# 11 — Self-learning from feedback

**Goal.** Check whether the assistant notices natural corrective feedback
and turns it into a gated self-improvement.

**Persona.** The single operator using the app casually, not someone who
knows the internal task labels, memory files, or improvement routines.

**What to try.** Ask for a quick casual suggestion, such as what to do with
a free Sunday. After the assistant answers, give plain negative feedback:
the answer was not useful, and next time you want one clarifying question
first and one concrete plan instead of a menu of options. Do not mention
self-improvement, memory, tasks, labels, or core memory.

**Done when.** The assistant has captured an `Improve the assistant` item
from the feedback, the item/task contains concrete evidence of what happened
and what was off, and any durable behavior/core-memory update waits for your
approval. Approve a concise behavior-memory update if one is proposed, then
verify it appears in Core memory and lightly check the behavior in a fresh
chat.

**Rate.** Use judgment. Note whether the assistant noticed the feedback
without being told the internal mechanism, whether the proposed learning was
appropriately general rather than overfit to the exact Sunday example, and
whether the approval/apply path was clear.
