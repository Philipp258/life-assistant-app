# 04 — Schedule a reminder

**Goal.** Ask the assistant to remind you about a real-world event at
a specific time, and confirm it has actually scheduled something — not
just acknowledged you in chat.

**Persona.** A user with a car-repair appointment on a specific
upcoming weekday. They want a reminder the morning before.

**Done when.** The assistant has created a task or reminder that you
can find in the app, dated for the correct time. End-to-end firing
(days later) is out of scope.

**Rate.** Whether the assistant understood the time correctly, whether
the created item is easy to locate in the UI, whether it looks like
something a user would trust to actually fire.
