# 11 — Explicit self-improvement request

**Goal.** Check that the assistant can handle self-improvement when the
user explicitly asks for it.

**Persona.** The single operator deliberately asking the assistant to
learn from a correction, because they want future behavior to change.

**What to try.** Ask for a quick casual suggestion, such as what to do with
a free evening. After the assistant answers, explicitly ask it to treat your
correction as an improvement: next time, ask whether you want something
active or restful first, then give one concrete plan.

**Done when.** The assistant creates or uses an `Improve the assistant`
item/task with concrete evidence, any durable behavior/core-memory update
waits for approval, and after approval the change appears in Core memory.
Lightly verify the behavior in a fresh chat.

**Rate.** Use judgment. Note whether the assistant understood the explicit
improvement request, whether the evidence and proposed learning were
appropriately general, and whether approval/apply felt clear.
