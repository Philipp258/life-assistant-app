# 03 — Simple chat

**Goal.** Have a short, normal back-and-forth conversation with the
assistant. Confirm the basic chat experience works end to end.

**Persona.** A user who just wants to ask the assistant a couple of
ordinary questions — something like "what should I cook tonight with
chicken and rice" followed by a follow-up.

**Done when.** You have exchanged at least three turns. Replies are
coherent and reference prior turns where they should.

**Rate.** Use judgment on the chat experience itself. Mention anything
that felt broken, confusing, or low-quality, such as rendering issues,
lost context, awkward handoffs, or unclear UI state. Do not rate
provider latency unless it creates a functional problem.
